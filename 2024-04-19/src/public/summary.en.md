UnJS brings together small, composable packages that make JavaScript development more portable and easier to extend. The talk moves from `ofetch` and `nypm` to `unbuild` and `changelogen`, showing how the ecosystem handles cross-runtime requests, package managers, builds, releases, and changelogs.

It then examines `defu`, `h3`, `nitro`, `std-env`, and `magic-regexp`. Together, these packages support recursive configuration, tree-shakable HTTP handlers, provider-aware deployment, environment detection, and readable regular expressions while remaining usable outside Nuxt.
