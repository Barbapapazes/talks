AI agents are changing how software work gets done, but automation does not remove the need for engineering judgment. This talk examines shifting bottlenecks, cognitive debt, the effect on junior developers, and the continued value of fundamentals such as understanding systems, checking generated work, and asking precise questions.

Rather than predict one fixed future, it asks developers to keep their agency. AI can handle more tasks, but people still need the technical depth and critical thinking to spot mistakes, choose between options, and act when the outcome is uncertain.
