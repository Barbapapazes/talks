This presentation takes a different look at Inertia by comparing explanations generated by an AI assistant with practical experience and live coding. It introduces Inertia's place between a backend and a frontend, discusses alternatives, and examines its main features.

The coding section focuses on the modern monolith approach, including server-side routes, frontend pages, links, forms, and optional props. The talk also asks what context and experience add when technical information is readily available from an assistant.
