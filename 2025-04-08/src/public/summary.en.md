Nuxt combines Vue.js and Nitro with built-in conventions for creating configurable and extensible full-stack applications. This talk starts with the structure of a Nuxt project, then looks at the needs of a web application and how Cloudflare's network and developer services address them.

NuxtHub connects these services to Nuxt through a module, a CLI, and a dashboard. The examples cover storage, databases, queues, deployment, logs, and data management.
